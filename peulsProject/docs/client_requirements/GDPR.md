# GDPR + Germany Compliance Notes (Developer Scope)

## Relevance for this project

This is relevant and required for this implementation. Shopify helps with infrastructure and templates, but does not automatically provide legally final compliance for a specific German/EU store setup.

## What I handle as developer (technical scope)

- Create and publish Privacy Policy page
- Add footer/navigation links to legal pages
- Configure newsletter/waitlist forms in a privacy-aware way
- Implement consent tooling (cookie banner/consent mode) when tracking is used
- Map third-party tools used on site (fonts, analytics, apps, embeds)
- Prepare technical documentation of where personal data is processed

## What is not developer legal responsibility

- Writing legal text from scratch as legal advice
- Guaranteeing full legal compliance/sign-off
- Acting as legal counsel for GDPR/German law interpretation

## Minimum legal pages/checkpoints for launch

- Privacy Policy (Datenschutzerklarung)
- Impressum (Germany)
- Cookie/consent information if non-essential cookies or trackers are active

## GDPR information points to cover in final legal text

- Data controller identity and contact details
- What data is collected
- Purpose and legal basis (GDPR)
- Recipients/processors and third countries/transfers
- Retention period/deletion criteria
- Data subject rights (access, deletion, objection, etc.)
- Complaint right with supervisory authority

## Shopify reality check

- Shopify provides legal templates and technical capabilities
- Shopify does not automatically adapt legal wording for your exact setup or all third-party apps
- Final wording should be reviewed by client-appointed legal counsel

## Client-safe wording to use

"I will implement the technical privacy setup (legal pages, links, consent tooling, and app/data mapping). Final legal wording and legal approval should be provided or validated by your legal advisor."

## Implementation checklist (Phase 1)

- [ ] Create Privacy Policy page in site structure
- [ ] Create Impressum page in site structure
- [ ] Add legal links in footer/navigation
- [ ] Add form notice text for newsletter/waitlist processing
- [ ] Confirm whether analytics/marketing scripts are active
- [ ] If active, implement consent banner and block non-essential scripts pre-consent
- [ ] List all third-party services currently in use
- [ ] Request legal copy review/approval from client before go-live
