## 1. Project

The Freelancer will create a Phase 1 brand website for Peuls Project and prepare the Shopify structure for a future ecommerce phase.

This agreement covers:
- Homepage
- About page
- Collection / story page
- Contact / newsletter section
- Shopify setup groundwork for future ecommerce activation
- Basic responsive testing

This agreement does not include, unless both sides approve it in writing later:
- Full ecommerce launch
- Payment gateway setup for live sales
- Shipping or tax configuration
- Advanced app automations
- Ongoing maintenance after handoff

---

## 2. Project Fee

The total fee for the work in this agreement is EUR 400.

Payment will be made in three milestones:
- Milestone 1: EUR 160 before implementation starts
- Milestone 2: EUR 160 after the staging version is approved by the Client
- Milestone 3: EUR 80 before final handoff or go-live

The Freelancer is not required to begin implementation before Milestone 1 is paid.

The Freelancer may pause work if a scheduled payment is late.

---

## 3. Timeline

The estimated timeline is 4 to 6 weeks from the date all required content, assets, and access are received.

This is an estimate, not a guaranteed fixed deadline.

If the Client delays approvals, content, feedback, or access, the timeline will move accordingly.

---

## 4. Client Responsibilities

The Client agrees to:
- Provide logos, text, images, product information, and brand materials needed for the project
- Review work and give feedback in a reasonable time
- Give platform access through approved methods only
- Pay each milestone on time

The Client will not share main account passwords with the Freelancer.

---

## 5. Shopify Access and Security

To protect both sides, platform access will be handled using Shopify's normal access methods.

If the Client already has a Shopify store:
- The Client will keep ownership of the store
- The Client will invite the Freelancer as collaborator or staff with limited permissions

If the Client does not yet have a Shopify store:
- The Freelancer may build the project in a Shopify Partner development store
- After approval and final payment, ownership will be transferred to the Client
- The Client will activate billing in their own name

The Freelancer will not ask for the Client's password.

---

## 6. Revisions

This agreement includes up to 2 rounds of reasonable revisions related to the agreed scope.

A revision means adjustments to existing agreed work. A revision does not mean new pages, new features, or a new direction.

Any extra revisions or new requests may require an additional quote, timeline update, or both.

---

## 7. Change Requests

If the Client asks for work outside the agreed scope, the Freelancer will explain the extra cost and timeline impact before starting that extra work.

Extra work will only begin after both sides approve it in writing.

---

## 8. Ownership of Work

Until the full project fee is paid, all design files, code, and working materials remain the property of the Freelancer.

After full payment is received, the Client will own the final deliverables created under this agreement.

The Freelancer may keep copies of the work for backup, recordkeeping, and portfolio use, unless the Client asks in writing for the work to remain confidential.

Third-party tools, platforms, fonts, apps, and external services remain the property of their respective owners and follow their own license terms.

---

## 9. Delivery and Handoff

Final handoff will take place after final payment.

At handoff, the Freelancer will provide the agreed final deliverables and a basic handoff package, which may include:
- Final approved theme or site files
- Store/theme transfer or collaborator handoff
- Basic notes for editing content
- List of major tools or apps used

If the Client wants additional training, maintenance, or support after handoff, that will require a separate agreement unless both sides state otherwise in writing.

---

## 10. Cancellation

Either side may cancel the project in writing.

If the Client cancels the project:
- Payments already made are not refunded for completed work
- The Client must pay for work completed up to the cancellation date

If the Freelancer cancels the project before completing the work:
- The Freelancer will deliver any work completed and paid for up to that point
- Any payment for work not yet started or not delivered will be refunded

---

## 11. Delays and Communication

Both sides agree to communicate clearly and in good faith.

If either side becomes unavailable or there is a serious delay, they should inform the other side as soon as possible.

If the Client does not respond for 14 consecutive days, the Freelancer may pause the project until communication resumes.

---

## 12. Limit of Responsibility

The Freelancer will perform the work with reasonable care and professionalism.

However, the Freelancer is not responsible for:
- Problems caused by third-party platforms or services
- Delays caused by missing assets, unclear instructions, or delayed feedback
- Losses related to business performance, sales, or indirect damages

The Freelancer's maximum responsibility under this agreement will not be more than the total amount actually paid by the Client under this agreement.
